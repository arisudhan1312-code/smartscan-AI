About SmartScan AI

Smart Scan Strategy for Electronic Warfare — an educational dashboard that shows how transparent prioritisation can help a human analyst decide which simulated signal samples to examine first.

SmartScan AI is an educational prototype using synthetic signal data. Unusual patterns are not proof of hostile activity. All recommendations require human review.

The problem

Signal analysts can face far more samples than they have time to inspect. Reviewing them strictly in arrival order means a small number of unusual samples may sit unnoticed behind hundreds of routine ones.

Proposed solution

Score every sample with a transparent, rule-based formula, flag unusual patterns, and present a ranked review queue — so the analyst sees the most unusual samples first while always making the final call.

Working principle

  1. STEP 01

    Simulate

    A seeded generator creates synthetic samples across three simulated bands. About 18% are deliberately given unusual traits so results can be checked against ground truth.

  2. STEP 02

    Normalise

    Signal strength, pattern variation, uncertainty and out-of-band frequency deviation are each scaled to a 0–1 feature.

  3. STEP 03

    Score

    A weighted sum of the four features produces a 0–100 priority score. The weights are visible and adjustable on the Signal Analysis page.

  4. STEP 04

    Flag & rank

    Threshold rules flag unusual patterns and explain why. Samples are sorted by score into the priority queue, which re-ranks every time new samples arrive.

  5. STEP 05

    Human review

    An analyst opens each sample, reads the explanation and recommended next step, and marks it reviewed. The system never takes action on its own.
